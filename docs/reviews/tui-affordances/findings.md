## Reconciliation and closure rules

The blind reader's predictions were collected before live effects. Keep an initial prediction even when the observed behavior differs; record the disagreement and its consequence instead of rewriting the prediction. Do not close an item from a source read alone. Required closure evidence is a durable capture or a documented reason no visual capture is possible, exact source path, live keyboard and mouse result where applicable, owner, and post-remediation verification. Current register is a triage sample; the ten route families and their states remain incomplete.
